Study Tips
GED Focus — 8 of 45 Done
You're 17.8% through your GED. Social Studies (17 left) and Physics (10 left) are the biggest mountains. Every PACE you tick now shortens that list permanently.
Speech — Due May 15 (Urgent)
Write your 2-minute speech on respect and kindness. Practice it out loud, time yourself, and record yourself on your phone. 2 minutes disappears fast if you're unprepared.
Book Report — Choose Now
Due June 12. Pick your 250-page book this week. Read 10 pages a day — that's 20 minutes. Finish the book by June 1 to give yourself time to write the report.
Wed & Fri Are Your Secret Weapons
No training on Wednesday. School ends at 1pm on Friday. Protect these windows every week and you will hit your PACE targets without burning out on school nights.
Use Your Goal Card Every Day
Every morning — pencil first, then pen. 4–6 pages per subject. Don't skip Checkups. You need 80%+ on the PACE Test to pass and tick it off for GED credit.
Sleep = Performance On Both Fields
On training nights (Mon/Tue/Thu) be in bed by 21:00. Your brain consolidates learning and your muscles recover while you sleep. Rest is not wasted time.
